# Other empirical studies: surveys, econometrics, social-science experiments

- **Design**: research question, hypotheses and whether they were preregistered (registry and link); deviations from the preregistration.
- **Sample**: population, sampling frame, response rate, exclusions and attention checks, final N per analysis.
- **Measures**: constructs and how they were measured; scale sources; reliability (for example Cronbach's alpha) and validity evidence; translation and back-translation when relevant.
- **Identification** (causal claims): the source of variation; assumptions (parallel trends, exclusion restriction, no manipulation at a cutoff) and the tests of them; placebo and falsification tests.
- **Estimation**: model specification; standard errors clustered at the level of treatment or sampling; fixed effects; how missing data were handled.
- **Robustness**: alternative specifications, samples and measures; how many were run and whether all are reported.
- **Multiple testing**: many outcomes or subgroups, with a correction or a prespecified primary outcome.
- **Effect sizes**: in meaningful units, with confidence intervals; not only significance.
- **Data and code**: availability statement; replication package.
- **Ethics**: approval and consent for human participants.

**Check especially**: N in the regression tables matches the sample description; the claimed mechanism is tested, not assumed; correlations are not described as effects.
