# Systematic reviews and meta-analyses (PRISMA 2020)

Official: PRISMA 2020 statement and checklist, www.prisma-statement.org.

**Title and abstract**
- Identified as a systematic review (and meta-analysis); abstract follows PRISMA for Abstracts.

**Introduction**
- Rationale in the context of existing knowledge; an explicit objective or question (PICO or similar).

**Methods**
- Eligibility criteria and how studies were grouped for synthesis.
- Every information source (databases, registers, websites, reference lists) with the date each was last searched.
- The full search strategy for all databases, including filters and limits.
- Selection process: how many reviewers screened each record, independently or not, and any automation tools.
- Data collection process, and the data items sought (outcomes and other variables), with assumptions for missing or unclear information.
- Risk-of-bias assessment: tool used, number of reviewers, independence.
- Effect measures for each outcome (risk ratio, mean difference ...).
- Synthesis methods: which studies were eligible for each synthesis, data preparation, tabulation, the statistical model and its rationale, heterogeneity assessment, sensitivity analyses.
- Assessment of reporting bias and of certainty in the body of evidence (for example GRADE).

**Results**
- Study selection with numbers at each stage (a flow diagram is expected), and studies that seemed eligible but were excluded, with reasons.
- Characteristics of each included study; risk of bias for each.
- Results of individual studies (summary statistics per group, effect estimate and precision), ideally in tables or forest plots.
- Results of syntheses: summary estimate with CI, heterogeneity measures, sensitivity analyses.
- Reporting biases and certainty of evidence for each outcome.

**Discussion**
- Interpretation in the context of other evidence; limitations of the evidence and of the review process; implications.

**Other**
- Registration (register name and number, for example PROSPERO) and protocol access; amendments.
- Funding, competing interests, and availability of data, code and other materials.

**Check especially**: the number of included studies is the same in abstract, flow diagram and tables; the search dates are stated; pooled estimates match the forest plot.
