# Animal research (ARRIVE 2.0)

Official: ARRIVE guidelines 2.0, arriveguidelines.org.

**The Essential 10**
1. Study design: the groups compared, including controls, and the experimental unit (animal, litter, cage).
2. Sample size: the number of experimental units per group and in total, and how it was decided (for example a power calculation).
3. Inclusion and exclusion criteria set in advance; any animals, units or data points excluded, with reasons; the exact number in each analysis.
4. Randomisation: whether and how animals were allocated at random; strategies to minimise confounders such as order of treatment or cage location.
5. Blinding: who knew group allocation at each stage (allocation, conduct, outcome assessment, analysis).
6. Outcome measures, with the primary outcome identified for hypothesis-testing studies.
7. Statistical methods for each analysis, software used, and how assumptions were checked.
8. Experimental animals: species, strain, sex, age or developmental stage, weight, source, genetic modification status.
9. Experimental procedures in detail: what was done, how, when, where, and why.
10. Results: for each analysis, summary statistics with variability, and the effect size with a confidence interval.

**Recommended set**
- Abstract; background; objectives; ethical approval statement with the committee and approval number.
- Housing and husbandry; animal care and monitoring, including humane endpoints and adverse events.
- Interpretation including limitations; generalisability and translation.
- Protocol registration; data access; declaration of interests.

**Check especially**: n per group is stated for every figure and test; the experimental unit is not confused with technical replicates; exclusions add up.
