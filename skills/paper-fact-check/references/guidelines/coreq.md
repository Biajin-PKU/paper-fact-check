# Qualitative research: interviews and focus groups (COREQ)

Official: COREQ checklist (Int J Qual Health Care 2007), www.equator-network.org.

**Research team and reflexivity**
- Who conducted the interviews or focus groups; their credentials, occupation, gender, experience and training.
- Any relationship with participants before the study; what participants knew about the researcher; researcher characteristics that could shape the findings (assumptions, interests).

**Study design**
- The methodological orientation (grounded theory, phenomenology, content analysis ...).
- Sampling (purposive, convenience, snowball); how participants were approached; the sample size; how many refused or dropped out and why.
- Setting of data collection; whether anyone else was present; description of the sample.
- The interview guide: whether it is provided and was pilot-tested; repeat interviews.
- Audio or visual recording; field notes; duration of interviews; whether data saturation was discussed; whether transcripts were returned to participants.

**Analysis and findings**
- Number of coders; description of the coding tree; whether themes were set in advance or derived from the data; software used; participant checking.
- Quotations presented and identified (participant number); consistency between data and findings; clarity of major and minor themes.

**Check especially**: the number of participants is the same throughout; quotations are attributed; claims are not generalised as if from a representative sample.
