# Randomised trials (CONSORT 2025)

Official: CONSORT 2025 statement and checklist, www.consort-spirit.org.

**Title and abstract**
- Title identifies the study as a randomised trial.
- Structured abstract: design, methods, results with numbers randomised and analysed, primary outcome with effect and precision, conclusions, registration, funding.

**Open science**
- Trial registration: registry name, number and date; whether registered before enrolment.
- Where the protocol and statistical analysis plan can be accessed.
- Data sharing: what, where, when, how.
- Funding sources and the role of funders; competing interests of authors.

**Introduction and methods**
- Scientific background, rationale, and specific objectives.
- Trial design (parallel, crossover, factorial ...) and allocation ratio; important changes after the trial started, with reasons.
- How patients or the public were involved in design, conduct or reporting.
- Settings and locations; eligibility criteria for sites and participants.
- Interventions for each group in enough detail to replicate, including how and when given.
- Prespecified primary and secondary outcomes: what, how and when measured; changes after the start, with reasons.
- Harms: how they were defined and assessed.
- Sample size: how it was determined; interim analyses and stopping rules.
- Randomisation: sequence generation, type (block, stratified), allocation concealment, who generated, enrolled and assigned.
- Blinding: who was blinded (participants, care providers, assessors, analysts) and how; similarity of interventions.
- Statistical methods for each outcome; the analysis population (intention to treat, per protocol); how missing data were handled; additional and subgroup analyses, prespecified or not.

**Results**
- Participant flow for each group: randomised, received intervention, analysed for the primary outcome; losses and exclusions with reasons (a flow diagram is expected).
- Recruitment and follow-up dates; why the trial ended or was stopped.
- Baseline characteristics per group (a table).
- Numbers analysed per group, and whether analysis was by original assigned groups.
- For each outcome: results per group, effect estimate and its precision (95% CI); absolute and relative effects for binary outcomes.
- Harms per group.
- Other analyses, labelled prespecified or exploratory.

**Discussion**
- Interpretation consistent with the results, balancing benefits and harms; limitations (bias, imprecision, multiplicity); generalisability.

**Check especially**: the primary outcome in the paper matches the registry; numbers in the flow diagram match the tables; subgroup claims rest on interaction tests, not on separate p-values.
